The thesis

Inference is a commodity. The data and the workflow are the moat.

Horizontal SaaS is being commoditized from both ends. Durable value moves to narrow tools that own a workflow and a dataset no one else has — and we build each one to graduate from an app into an agent.

01

Vertical, not horizontal

Win the micro-segment the platforms skip. Shipping fast into one under-served niche is far more likely to reach profitability.

02

Proprietary data moat

Every product collects the one dataset that compounds in its vertical — labeled, longitudinal, in-workflow. That's the asset, not the features.

03

Workflow depth

We own the moment where money leaks — the quote, the claim, the close — not a generic wrapper around a model.

04

Ambient endgame

"Opening an app" will feel outdated by 2036. Each product is designed to become an always-on agent. The app is the wedge.
